A gate enforces only that a named check ran and reported a non-neutral pass — it does not make the check correct. Correctness is the mutation test's job: a guard that has never gone red is indistinguishable from one that does nothing, so unproven guards are counted separately and refused until proven.

Where it's going

Toward a version-scoped, annual conformance audit that earns the Valiant Verification mark, with the unproven-guard backlog closed to zero and the SDK and corpus published for implementers.
